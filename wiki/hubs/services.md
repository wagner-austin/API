# Services

The FastAPI ML/NLP/media services that make up the api platform surface. Each service runs on its own port with a per-service README as the front door — this hub covers cross-service architecture, shared patterns, and subsystem-depth pages that expand on what each README opens with. Services: data-bank-api (8001, content-addressed storage), Model-Trainer (8005, LM training), Art-Trainer (8011, image LoRAs), handwriting-ai (8004, MNIST), turkic-api (8000, Turkic language detection + IPA), transcript-api (8003, YouTube transcription), qr-api (8002), music-wrapped-api (8006), covenant-radar-api (8007, risk prediction + Kafka), grandma-api (8008, multi-language audio-to-English), github-stats-api (8009, SVG cards), opportunity-radar-api (8010, hackathon discovery), procart-api (port not yet fixed).

[Art-Trainer publishes LoRAs another repo consumes](../pages/art-trainer-publishes-loras-another-repo-consumes.md) -- the LoRA trainer (Kohya-ss, SD1.5/SDXL/FLUX, BLIP+Gemini+GPT-4o captioning, Redis+RQ) and the seam nothing records: it deploys adapters by COPYING them into ComfyUI's models directory, so there is no import, no HTTP call and no shared package between it and its consumer in the separate `chat` repository -- the coupling is a directory on disk, invisible to either dependency graph, and until 2026-09-11 neither README named the other, which is why `chat`'s own roadmap listed "LoRA training pipeline" as MISSING while this service shipped; also why it is absent from `docs/RESEARCH.md` (runs/ empty, no RunRecord -- it produces artifacts, not numbers anyone subtracts) and what the consumer checks that this service cannot (ComfyUI validates a LoRA file EXISTS but not that its family matches the checkpoint, so a mismatched adapter loads and silently discards every weight)

[Covenant Radar's backend registry](../pages/covenant-radar-backend-registry.md) -- one interface over seven classifiers and five regressors, split across `covenant_ml` and `covenant_nn`; why the `Literal` is canonical and not either registry; and the README count that drifted one low

[Attaching a corpus to a model](../pages/corpus-attachment-program.md) -- the program index over four arms at one question: weight injection (no, and now MEASURED rather than argued -- hand-curated triples clear a seven-clause grounding gate 13 times in 20, and across EIGHT configurations, two edit depths x four scales, every accepted edit lands and not one rung answers more than a single extra item; gpt2-xl is flat at 0.5625 through thirteen edits at each depth, twenty-six in all), steering vectors (no -- nothing left at four composed vectors), cartridges (YES against the UN-AUGMENTED BASE from ~774M -- 0.8021 and 0.8229 at large and xl against 0.5312 and 0.5625, +8.7 and +8.3 items of 32, while costing less per request than dense and fused everywhere; NOT established against retrieval, and the claim that it beat lexical, dense and fused was retracted on the day it was published, because 1.7 and 1.3 items of 32 are below the 6-item net difference McNemar exact can ever resolve at that n), persona adapter (untested); carries TWO of its own retractions -- the one-day negative verdict from an instrument that examined nine of the twelve pages it trained on and still passed 100% coverage, and the same-day positive one that struck a caveat on the first evidence pointing the desired way without computing whether the instrument could see a difference that size -- plus the retraction of the un-headlined "dense retrieval LOSES to keyword search" finding, whose margins are 1, 3, 1 and 1 items and which the same floor rejects -- what survives that comparison is the ORACLE GAP, 6 to 8 items at every rung, saying both retrievers miss something without saying which misses more

[Cartridge composition ceiling](../pages/model-trainer-composition-ceiling.md) -- Model-Trainer's composition-scaling sweep: two compartments is the measured limit (63% retention at n2, negative by n4, erased by n8, bit-identical record), the cost flips from structural to content interference with scale, and the cross-gain arm caught its own roster's leakage

[Cartridge question set](../pages/model-trainer-cartridge-question-set.md) -- the arm that asks whether a cartridge's knowledge is USABLE rather than merely less surprising, and TWO corrections pointing opposite ways: the 24-item instrument examined nine of the twelve pages it trained on and reported +0.0972 inside a seed spread of 0.1250, the corrected 32-item one reports +0.1667 against a spread of 0.0313 -- and then the seed spread turned out to be the wrong yardstick entirely, because the arms answer the SAME 32 items and McNemar exact governs, whose floor is 6 net items (the page's own p-values give it away, BM25's +10 items reporting exactly 2*(0.5)^10 = 0.0020); +0.1667 is 6/5/5 items across seeds, so the 124M accuracy result sits ON the floor and "it loses to all three retrievers at 124M" is struck at 4.7 items; the +7.81 answer-NLL gain is continuous and unaffected; the distractor policy alone flipped the base between chance and 0.5417, and two runs of one plan were indistinguishable until the question set got a payload digest

[A declared batch size trained at four times itself](../pages/model-trainer-declared-config-is-not-a-suggestion.md) -- the worker silently rewrote any declared batch of 4 or less on CUDA, and every record kept saying what the payload declared

[The noise floor is a range](../pages/model-trainer-noise-floor-is-a-range.md) -- `noise_floor` is `max(gains) - min(gains)` maxed over arms, so it grows with the seed count (0.0202 at 3 seeds, 0.0546 at 9 -- same corpus, same code) and discards the seed pairing the sweep's steps are made of; replicating three times harder made the run reject THREE of four steps including one it had accepted, while the paired statistics show all four are real (t = 21.7 / 12.7 / 12.3 / 4.8) and gpt2 keeps gaining to 512 slots with no saturation in range

[Companioned training recipe](../pages/model-trainer-companioned-training-recipe.md) -- the intervention that moves the composition ceiling: train every cartridge with a frozen held-out companion at p=0.5 and four-compartment retention goes from -45.4% to +44.6% (bit-identical record, 15x its floor) for a solo cost of four hundredths, with a dose curve and an overdose endpoint in both companion kinds; the arc ends with the base itself trained to read crowds twice over: the LM-objective LoRA solves the structural half at both scales, and crowd-invariance distillation closes the content half at depth (medium n8 -79.4% -> +38.1%, n4 +63.2% the program record; eight compartments deliverable on both bases, both levers base-side)

[The fine-tune learned the house style and forgot how to stop](../pages/code-style-qlora-terminates-worse-conforms-better.md) -- a QLoRA adapter over Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B trained on this monorepo passes MORE of the repo's own guards than its base on files it finishes (72.0% -> 78.4%, +18, mid-p 0.020) and finishes 23.4 points fewer of them (64.5% -> 41.1%); the aggregate reads as uniform degradation because truncation fails ruff on syntax, and the combined three-checker rate is null at a 3% floor

[Measuring a disposition needs a different dependent variable](../pages/model-trainer-trait-composition-instrument.md) -- the trait-composition instrument, built because every compartment this arc had composed was a CORPUS and a persona has no held-out corpus in that sense: each arm is scored as a PREFERENCE between two continuations of one prompt and the arms are differenced, so a prefix that lowers loss everywhere cancels and no judge model enters the path; the same cancellation is what blinds it to a cartridge that expresses a trait by becoming worse at everything, which is why a COHERENCE reading is derived from the same four losses and cannot be omitted; the published steering-vector intervention runs as its own arm at matched trait counts, and because nothing is drawn its rows are named `_once` rather than `_mean`; two refusals run before the hours -- the power gate on the REALISED held-out pair count and the solo precondition BETWEEN cells, which is the step the 7B rung failed while its composition numbers were read for days; no cluster run yet, and the diverse-companion, base-LoRA and crowd-invariance families are deliberately absent because a repair is only askable once there is a naive interference number to repair
