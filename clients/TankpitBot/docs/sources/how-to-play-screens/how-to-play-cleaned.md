# How To Play

---

## The Basics

- **Click** on a land tile and your tank will attempt to drive there.
- **Double click** (or click on) an enemy tank to shoot it.
- **Click on** enemy mines to blow them up.
- **Click and hold** to grab or drop an obstacle. Tow an obstacle behind your tank and use it to build a bridge or base.
- **Click and hold** to grab equipment.
- **Move to fuel** to pick it up. Don't let your tank run out of fuel!
- **Use a ferry** to drive on water.
- Enemy tanks will be blocked and damaged if they drive into your mines.

---

## Controls

- **Map:** Open the map, then click on it to teleport your tank to a new location.
- **Radar:** Reveal hidden fuel, equipment, or enemy mines around you.
- **Mine:** Plant mines.
- **Scope:** Change your viewport.
- **Equipment icons:** Click to enable or disable your equipment. Hover to see your inventory.
- **Fuel/Promotion bar:** View your progress towards promotion. If you run out of fuel, your tank will be deactivated!

---

## Equipment

| Equipment | Description |
|---|---|
| Armor Shields | Protect your tank from enemy fire and conserve fuel. |
| Dual Shots | Inflict double the damage on the enemy. |
| Missile Shots | Fire over mountains, obstacles, or other tanks in the way. |
| Homing Shots | Follow an enemy as they drive or teleport away. |
| Extra Radars | Scan the entire viewport instead of just the area around your tank. |

Recruits can hold 20 of each equipment type. Each higher rank can hold 5 more of each equipment type than the previous rank. Equipment use can be enabled or disabled.

---

## Ranks

| Rank | Promotion Requirements |
|---|---|
| Recruit | This is the lowest and the starting rank. |
| Private | Earn 500 promotion points. |
| Corporal | Earn 1,000 promotion points. |
| Sergeant | Earn 4,000 promotion points. |
| Lieutenant | Earn 10,000 promotion points + deactivate a corporal or higher. |
| Captain | Earn 20,000 promotion points + deactivate a sergeant or higher. |
| Major | Earn 30,000 promotion points + deactivate a lieutenant or higher. |
| Colonel | Earn 40,000 promotion points + deactivate a captain or higher. |
| General | Earn 50,000 promotion points + deactivate a colonel or higher. |

Higher rank tanks hold more fuel, equipment, and have a larger radar. If your tank is deactivated by an enemy, you will be demoted one rank.
