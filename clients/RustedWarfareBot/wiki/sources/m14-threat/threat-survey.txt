map [z;p10]Crossing Large (10p), agent attached on channelPort=7712.

Method: connect over the agent channel, take one sample, send nothing, and run
the planner's own survey against it. This asks the rule what it would say
about the real map from the builder's real position, rather than acting on it.

Reading:

Tile (12, 52) is the pool that got a builder killed -- 4,272 units from the
anchor, the farthest on the map. It now reads exposed as well as occupied, and
the two are independent: the straight route to it passes through fire whether
or not something is standing on it.

The filter costs nothing in the opening. Old rule and new rule pick the same
tile at 694 units, because the ground near the base is clear. It only starts
removing options once the near pools are taken, which is exactly when the old
rule started reaching across the map.

It does not cap distance. Fourteen pools are free and safe here and the
farthest is 2,518 units out, so a long walk is still permitted -- it is only
chosen when every nearer pool is gone, since distance still ranks the
survivors.

The counts drift while the sample is being taken, because the opponents are
playing. An earlier probe on the previous run of the same map read 2 rejected
and 27 safe at 34,673 frames; this one reads 11 and 14. What reproduces across
both is the shape: the near picks are unaffected, the far pools are the ones
that fall out, and the pool from the recorded death is always among them.
