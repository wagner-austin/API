Anti-air in the army: does closing the air gap change the outcome?

Twelve seeds per arm at each of two rungs on [p2]duel_lake, 4,000 samples. The
arms differ only in the goals string -- c_tank x4 against c_tank x3 + hoverTank
-- which is where the army mix comes from, so no code changed ([[policy-production]]).

WHY THE HOVERTANK AND NOT THE TURRET. c_antiAirTurret is the better unit (7.50
dps per 100 credits against 3.41, 133 hp per 100 against 33, 250 range) but a
BUILDER places it, and the builder channel built three turrets across twelve
full matches. The hovertank comes off a Land Factory through the production
channel, which reinforced 120-143 units in the same runs.

READING THE ENGAGEABLE COLUMN. Compare only matches that did NOT finish. A win
ends with a handful of enemies left and often reads 0% -- that is the bot having
won while unable to shoot the aircraft that survived it, not a failure.

                    won   lost      drops   engageable, in matches that ran on
  duel-hard         4/12      0        21   76-83%
  duel-hard-aa      6/12      1        16   89-98%
  duel-medium       9/12      0         -   84-91%
  duel-medium-aa   10/12      1         -   96-100%

The drops column is blank for any batch whose traces were later overwritten;
see compare.py, which refuses to borrow another run's trace.

VERDICT: the mechanism is confirmed -- the army can now shoot what it sees --
and the win gain is real but small, 13/24 -> 16/24 across both rungs, which is
not decisive at that count. Both new losses carry the stalled-plan signature
(army zero beside a large banked balance) rather than a combat one, and the same
signature appeared in the defence arm too, so it predates this change.
