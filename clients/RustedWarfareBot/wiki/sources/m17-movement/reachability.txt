Land connectivity of the archived map's resource pools
=====================================================

Game: Rusted Warfare 1.15 (code 176, build #28), map [z;p10]Crossing Large (10p).
Derived from wiki/sources/m6-wire/world-sample.ndjson, which carries the
engine's own connectivity component per entity and per pool. Regenerable from
that capture alone -- no running game.

The engine precomputes connected components per movement layer and reduces
reachability to comparing two component ids. Negative ids are not ids: -1 is
impassable, -2 is off the map, -3 is 'the grids were never built for this
layer'.

builder movement=LAND group=1
pools by land component: 1:34, 16:2, 17:2, 18:2, 19:2, 22:2, 24:2
reachable by this builder: 34/46
cut off: (115, 6), (133, 18), (90, 40), (102, 46), (142, 60), (133, 65), (90, 158), (101, 165), (143, 187), (135, 191), (98, 217), (84, 223)
  owned commandCenter    movement=NONE     group=-3
  owned builder          movement=LAND     group=1
  owned editorOrBuilder  movement=LAND     group=-2

Reading:

Twelve of the forty-six pools sit in six two-pool components the mainland
cannot walk to. On a symmetric ten-player map that reads as six island pairs.
Distance-only selection would have sent a land builder at one of them as soon
as the near ground filled up, and the order would have been accepted and never
completed.

Two independent confirmations of the sentinel meanings fall out of the same
sample. The Command Center reports movement NONE and group -3: a building does
not move, so no layer grid covers it. The map editor's placeholder is parked at
(-1000, -1000) and reports LAND with group -2 -- exactly the off-map sentinel,
from a unit independently known to be off the map.
