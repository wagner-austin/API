The difficulty ladder after the economy fixes, baseline beside result.

Twelve seeds per rung on [p2]duel_lake, 4,000 samples, one opponent. Difficulty
is an income multiplier on the AI alone: 1.0x Medium, 1.4x Hard, 1.8x Very Hard,
3.7x Impossible.

  Medium           1.0x      fixed   12/12     0      6        2013         13.6x
  Hard             1.4x   baseline    4/12     0      0        2475             -
  Hard             1.4x      fixed    6/12     0      2        2080          0.2x
  Very Hard        1.8x   baseline    5/11     1      1        2907          0.0x
  Very Hard        1.8x      fixed    7/12     2      3        2207          0.0x

"routs" counts matches leaving the opponent with NOTHING; the margin column
reads "total" for those. No baseline at any rung produced a single one.

MEDIUM IS NOW PERFECT: twelve of twelve, no losses, six routs, and **the worst
match beats the baseline's median** (13.6x against 9.8x). The largest extractor
loss in any of the twelve was three.

WHAT THE FIXES WERE, in the order they were found -- the first is instrumentation
and it is what made the rest visible at all:
  * the budget ledger and the spender census (nothing reported what the bot was
    trying to buy, or which spender was even asked)
  * the expander gate -- 572 of 800 samples had been skipped entirely
  * pools claimed in flight -- 23 orders had been buying 4 extractors
  * the priced-out gate -- 29 turrets had been bought against 4 extractors
  * the economy floor -- 2,800 credits of 65,000 had been reaching the economy

READ THE DROPS COLUMN. At Very Hard the separation is exact and has no overlap:
every win lost four extractors or fewer, every non-win lost six or more. At Hard
it is strong but not clean (one seed survived on two drops), so "extractor losses
decide the match" is the dominant effect rather than the whole of it.

A CAUTION ON READING THE RUNGS AGAINST EACH OTHER: Very Hard (7/12) scoring above
Hard (6/12) on identical code is the noise floor talking. Twelve seeds cannot
separate 50% from 58% in a harness whose engine randomness is unseeded
([[policy-determinism]]). Treat the two as indistinguishable.
