THE GUIDE
step 1. built sh!!!ttons of fabricators, trust me, it'll be a worthy investment later down the line

step 2. build considerable invasion force, 100-300 tanks will do

step 2.a: if your on an island map use missile ships (don't use landing craft even if they are cheaper)

step 3. unleash le army (make sure to send le builders along to claim any resource nodes on the way)

step 4. send constant stream of tanks onto the frontline as you prepare your bigger units for battle in a big f%cking blob of units

if step 3/4 doesn't work: use leftover credits to build le nuclear silos and saturate bomb them (provided you've played smart and upgraded le fabricators)

alternatively: repeat step 2 until it works

step 5. unleash le experimental units to destroy unusually stubborn AI bases (or if you want to be overkill like the bastard you are)

step 6. win
