[the 2v1 post] ...You are down a command center firepower; 18 income less;
and 4000 less starting credits... you must manage to gain a +4500 credit
worth of combat exchange rate for starting units and another +2400 credit
exchange rate... a 23 small tank worth of positive exchange rate...
1. Turret rush: Turrets offer excellent exchange rate if they're built in
   the first place. Try drag battle within Turret fire and dismantle them
   after the battle ends.
2. Land forces commanding: Use centralized fire tactic and retreat hurt
   units tactic to optimize your combat exchange rate.
3. Air forces commanding: Use beaconing and flank tactic...
4. Raiding: Destroying defenseless building is pure exchange rate gaining.
   Sneak attack vulnerable sites and retreat to gain positive exchanges.
5. Counter exploit: use your info advantages to counter enemy units.
   Counter units offer great exchange rates.
...Avoid late game and end it during mid-game with advantages you've
managed to gain.

[turret-rush counters] Small tanks opening to catch unprotected builders...
If they upgrade a turret to artillery, it's a big, big mistake... artillery
tower dps is catastrophically low... build a gun turret on its face...
If they upgrade to a T2 gun: use land units and concentrated forces...
If they build a Maginot line: Minigun mech can obliterate all defences
without T3 gun or T2 lightning, its range is a little bit longer. If T3 gun
is applied, avoid that area entirely.

[rush-vs-2 advice] ...deny an extractor spot or kill an extractor and you
take the initiative. Put a scout unit between your base and theirs to be
alerted early... and between their bases to see if one reinforces the
other, then raid the one who isn't reinforced. Knowledge is power...
Do your build a few times in single-player... speed is everything.

[impossible turtle account] "defense is your greatest offense" -- capture
as many extractor points as I can before the ai gets to them; mid game up
the defense and turrets of those extractors while building fabricators;
slowly conquer enemy extractors 1 by 1...

[100+ hour balance review]
Amphibious Jet: bread-and-butter spammable, scales nonlinearly (15 easy,
30 tough, 40+ overwhelming), not hard-countered by anything -- to kill
them you build Amph Jets yourself.
Machine Gun Mech: long range + fast gun (hard-counters laser defense),
no air defense, weak against spammed units.
Gun T3, artillery T2: part of the problem with overly strong bases. Five
together take tremendous effort; a base with 10 -- ughhh.
Nautilus, Heavy Air Defense Mech, Plasma Mecha, Hovertanks, shielded
hovertanks: why do these exist? Never seen them used successfully.
Heavy Tank: another versatile spammable unit, more balanced than jets.
Particle Beam fighters: not good for their price.
Bomber: supposed anti-turtle, doesn't do it well, too expensive.
Flak cannon: falls short in practice.
Fire bee: spammed fairly effectively to counter heavy defenses.
Flying fortress: versatile and fun.
Experimental Tank: a "win harder" unit, not a tide changer.
Spider: walking nuke play inside anti-nuke zones; modules get scary but
all-in expensive.
