Fabricator T2
Alternative building for generating Credits. The primary building for this purpose is the 
Extractor
. As the Extractor, it can be upgraded twice.
The Fabricator differs from the Extractor in that it can be built anywhere on the map, which apart from being easier to defend also means that it can be built in almost unlimited quantity, whereas Extractors are restricted to Resource Pools.
However, due to their far lower efficiency and steep cost, it is advisable to only build these late in the game, when all available Extractors have been taken and upgraded, and a stalemate on the fronts makes additional income desirable.
