This is a general worker unit that is used consistently from the beginning to the end. There is no attack function, and since there are buildings in this game that extract resources separately, resources are not collected.
[1]
 Engineers only build buildings as intended, and can also repair and dismantle buildings. 
On servers where team control is enabled, you can freely dismantle buildings belonging to users on the same team.
If an engineer constructing a defense line dies, a catastrophe could occur, so it is always best to be careful. Moreover, since it is one of the glass-bodied units in the game along with the self-propelled artillery, 
it is killed in one hit by the experimental spider robot's self-propelled artillery.
A unit repair function also existed during the Alpha days, but was nerfed due to balance issues, and Combat Engineers inherited this function.

A hover unit that can build and attack. It is a superior compatible unit that complements all the shortcomings of a regular engineer. While the above engineer cannot attack other units, this combat engineer can attack other units. Of course, the cost-effectiveness is not very good, so it is better to focus on the fact that it can defend to some extent against reconnaissance vehicles and hover tanks. It can move on water and even shoot torpedoes!
[3]
 You can set it to start with 3 of these before starting the game.
Also, unlike regular engineers, they can heal units and create T2 extractors, T2 ground/air/sea/robot factories, heavy tanks, and amphibious jets right away, so they are often acquired around mid-game and used to fill out tech tree buildings or extractors. It has an automatic repair function. Produces +1$ per unit.
In the past, it was possible to build T2 Collector and T2 Robot Factory, but due to balance issues, T2 Robot Factory was inherited by Robot Engineer, and T2 Collector was deleted and replaced with Watchtower Construction.
Because it can repair units, it produces a lot of combat engineers and shows excellent performance for the purpose of supporting experimental units.

A unit that can travel on land and water. Aerial attacks are possible, and if you put them in a group at the opponent's base, you can see the opponent's turret being destroyed. However, their physical strength is very weak, so when attacking in groups, be careful of flamethrowers and wide-area units that use wide-area attacks.
Along with the scout car, it is the AI ​​player's main unit. 
Also, it
 is faster when moving on water than on land. The same goes for heavy hover tanks and landing craft.
It is a unit that boasts good cost-effectiveness even for reconnaissance. It is cheaper than a reconnaissance car, has a quick production time, and is free to move, so it boasts excellent performance for the purpose of checking sappers who are constructing reconnaissance and expansion bases rather than using it as a full force. Because of this, it is currently a unit that is being re-evaluated in multiplayer.

Wide Area Unit/Building
Hover tanks have the 2nd lowest health among ground units.
[7]
 Because it possesses it, it shows all units/buildings in a wide area being oxidized in less than 2 seconds. Therefore, be careful, because if you only compete based on the number of hover tanks, the moment your opponent draws wide-area units, the tide may turn.
tank, basic robot
Although they are units produced at the same time, they are very superior in performance compared to hover tanks. In the case of tanks, 4 tanks take down 5 hover tanks, the default robot is a higher-level tank unit, and because of its excellent health and attack speed, the 3 base robots take out all 6 hover tanks without losing a single one. do.
Self-Propelled Artillery, Enhanced Self-Propelled Artillery, Artillery Robot, Mobile Turret
Since these units perform ranged attacks, 
they are truly fatal units against hover tanks with weak health.
 The self-propelled howitzer has the same weak health as the hover tank, but its range is very long, so the hover tank is reversed, the artillery robot has high health and a wide damage range, and the reinforced self-propelled howitzer has a built-in laser shield that nullifies attacks twice and has higher stamina than existing self-propelled guns. In the case of a mobile turret, when switched to turret mode, the entire hover tank melts due to its high defense and fast rate of fire.
