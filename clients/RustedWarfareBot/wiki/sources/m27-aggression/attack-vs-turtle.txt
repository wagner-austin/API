Is attacking what kills the bot?

Every loss is spent on offence -- 96% of losses fall more than 2,000 world units
from home, none within 900 of it -- and 200 to 300 units a match are fed in for a
few thousand credits of damage. So the question is whether the bot does better
by never attacking at all.

It needed no code. The wave ladder's final rung is already an argument, and a
mass larger than any army the bot can field releases no wave ever. The army
still gathers, still rallies at the base, and still defends; it simply never
walks into the opponents' half.

Two arms, three seeds, six matches, one batch, played to a verdict.
