Baseline this replaces, from the original audit: army 4 -> 2, three extractors
frozen for the whole match, 21,164 credits banked, and no way to compare against
an opponent at all.

Artillery loses decisively, and the engine's own catalogue said so before the
runs did: 2.22 damage per second per 100 credits against the tank's 5.71. Its
290 range does out-reach the c_turret_t1 that our tanks cannot trade with, and
that did not compensate.

Variance collapsed: 40,850 / 41,200 / 40,850 across three seeds, against a
documented 3 / 6 / 14 army spread from identical code before this work. That is
the multi-builder change removing a bottleneck the bot used to hit at a
different point in every run.

NOT ESTABLISHED: whether we are killing anything. players 5 -> 5 in all six
runs, so no opponent was ever eliminated. Army value is also flattered by
builders, which are mobile and count toward it while doing no fighting -- 28,050
across 33 fighting units is roughly 850 each against a tank's 350. Rival worth
is recorded only at the last observation, so nothing here can say whether an
opponent's value ever dipped.
