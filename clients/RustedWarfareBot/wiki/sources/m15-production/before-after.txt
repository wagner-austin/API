Continuous production, measured against the sortie it replaced
==============================================================

Same map, same opening goals, same sandbox skirmish against five AI opponents.
The only difference is whether idle producers keep making the units the goals
asked for once the plan is finished.

WITHOUT reinforcement (wiki/sources/m14-ai era, runs/fight.log)
  fight outcome  no_army
  attack orders  29
  army           4 -> 0
  enemies seen   52 -> 86
  engaged gone   7
  samples seen   466        <- the run ended here because the army was gone

WITH reinforcement (this run, sustained-run.log)
  fight outcome  sample_limit
  attack orders  743
  reinforced     45
  army           4 -> 2
  enemies seen   47 -> 142
  engaged gone   14
  samples seen   1500       <- the run ended on the budget, not on defeat

Read three things out of this.

1. It survives. The army is no longer wiped: the phase ran to its 1500-sample
   budget instead of ending at 466 with nothing left. Twice as many engaged
   targets went missing (14 against 7).

2. It is still losing. Army 4 -> 2 means losses outrun replacements, and the
   opponents went 47 -> 142 over the same window. Reinforcement bought
   survival, not parity.

3. A new problem is now visible: target churn. 743 attack orders were issued
   across 48 distinct attacking units against only 24 distinct targets --
   roughly fifteen re-orders per unit. Targets are chosen as nearest-to-army-
   centre, and that centre moves every time a unit dies or a new one rolls out,
   so the whole army is re-tasked on a flip that may be a few world units wide.

   The engine's own AI does not do this. It holds a target and refreshes on an
   800 ms timer rather than on change (wiki: ai-opponent-strategy), which is
   the same order volume spread over a stable choice instead of a thrashing
   one.

Counts verified from the run log:
  produce orders dispatched  49   (4 from the build plan, 45 from reinforcement)
  attack orders dispatched  743
  distinct targets           24
  distinct attackers         48
