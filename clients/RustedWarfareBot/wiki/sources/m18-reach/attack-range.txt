--- why -printunits stops at 90 ---

ar.s() filters: not an orderable unit; name starts with 'bug'; shadowed by
another type; a custom type without its listing flag; one specific excluded
type; and an explicit sixteen-name blocklist. The '--- ERROR: running
printForHelp()' lines in the log are not errors -- it is a fifty-iteration
banner loop at the top of the same method.

Reading:

Not exotic. Most of the 27 are the SAME unit in another mode, and the mode is
the type name a live entity reports while in it: a deployed mech bunker
(240), a surfaced submarine (240), a landed experimental gunship (390), an
amphibious jet underwater (100). Under the old source every one of those read
as unarmed. Add the bug faction on any map with bug players (bugRangedT2 at
270, bugTurret at 210) and the modular spider tree (up to 400).

--- what it changed on one live sample ---

Nothing, and that is worth recording too. Of 55 visible hostiles on
[z;p10]Crossing Large, 20 read as armed under either source and the pool
survey was identical: occupied=15 unreachable=12 exposed=1, same tile chosen.
The five AI players had built only priced types by that point. The fix removes
a latent hole rather than a firing one -- but the hole is ordinary unit states,
not a hypothetical.
