What the bot does after its plan completes
==========================================

Every run before this one stopped the moment the plan finished, which measures
whether the bot can execute a list -- not whether it can play. This observes
only: no orders are sent, and the world is sampled for 800 samples (~30,000
engine frames) after a completed six-entry plan on the sandbox skirmish map,
five opposing AI players.

START  frame  51046 credits  8539 mine 9 hp 178190 visible-enemy  54
  t+ 200 frame  66050 credits 11689 mine 9 hp 178190 visible-enemy  63
  t+ 400 frame  81058 credits 14843 mine 9 hp 178190 visible-enemy  81
  t+ 600 frame  96032 credits 18001 mine 9 hp 178190 visible-enemy 102
  t+ 800 frame 110949 credits 21164 mine 9 hp 178190 visible-enemy 126
END    owned 9 -> 9

Three readings, and the third is the one that matters.

1. Nothing was lost. Owned entities held at 9 and total hit points did not move
   by a single point -- so the bot took no damage at all, rather than surviving
   damage. (170,000 of that total is the editor placeholder's; the real figure
   is about 8,190, equally unchanged.)

2. Credits rose from 8,539 to 21,164 and never stopped. The economy works and
   nothing spends it. A bot banking 21,000 credits is a bot with three
   extractors it does not need and an army it never built.

3. Visible enemy units went from 54 to 126 over the same window. The opponents
   more than doubled their force while the bot stood still.

So "it survived" is true and misleading. It was not attacked, on a map large
enough that five opponents had not reached it in five minutes. Nothing here
shows the bot can take a hit, return one, or notice it is being approached --
only that the failure has not arrived yet.
