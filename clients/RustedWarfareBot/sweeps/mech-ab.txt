# mechGun against c_tank, paired across twelve seeds.
#
# label | seed | goals | max_workers | samples | mass
#
# A PAIRED design, because the noise floor forces it. Twelve runs of one
# identical specification gave 3 survivals and 9 defeats with total worth
# spanning 350 to 15,350 -- and seeding java.lang.Math's generator did not
# shrink it ([[policy-determinism]]). So arms are compared per seed, and scored
# on `samples seen` (how long the bot lasted), which is the lowest-variance
# figure the scorecard carries: standard deviation about 300 against a mean
# near 3,600, where total worth has a standard deviation larger than its own
# mean.
#
# One candidate rather than a twelve-way screen. At this noise level a
# one-match-per-arm screen would report roughly three survivals whatever the
# arms were, so the twelve compositions in sweeps/comps.txt wait until there is
# something worth screening for.
#
# Why mechGun: it dominates c_tank on both axes at identical reach -- 600
# credits for 500 hit points and 7.67 damage per second per 100 credits,
# against 350 for 210 and 5.71. That is 83 hit points per 100 credits against
# 60 ([[mechanics-unit-value]]). Naming it makes expansion insert a
# mechFactory, so the arm also carries a different producer.
